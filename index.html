Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored? +

On your device by default. If you turn on cloud backup or account-based sync, the data those features need is sent to the provider behind them.

Does Lago connect to my bank? +

No. Lago never asks for banking credentials and requires no bank connection. You enter transactions yourself, and the app works fully offline.

Is it on both iOS and Android? +

Yes. Lago is on the App Store and Google Play, and optional sync keeps eligible data aligned across both.

How do I delete my account and cloud data? +

Turning sync off or signing out does not remove data already in the cloud. Submit a verified request on the Account & Data Deletion page.
